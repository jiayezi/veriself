# 指标清单 v1（18 个，冻结）

> 口径来源：`metrics/*.yml`。字段含义见 `docs/00-接口契约.md` 第 2 节。

## A. 睡眠与恢复（7 个）

| metric_id | grain | agg | unit | direction | 数据来源 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `subject.sleep_duration_daily` | day | mean | hour | higher_better | `fact_observation.sleep_hours`(channel=sleep_hours) | 当日睡眠时长 |
| `subject.sleep_need_deviation_daily` | day | mean | hour | neutral | 上一指标 + `dim_subject.sleep_need_h` | 实际 − 需求 |
| `subject.sleep_debt_7d` | day | mean | hour | lower_better | upstream: `sleep_need_deviation_daily` | 近 7 日缺口之和（滚动窗口） |
| `subject.sleep_regularity_7d` | day | mean | score | higher_better | upstream: `sleep_duration_daily` | 近 7 日睡眠时长标准差映射到 0-100：`clamp(100 − 40σ, 0, 100)`（σ 单位小时；系数 40 按合成数据实测标定，σ 中位数 ≈0.80h → 中位分 ≈68） |
| `subject.recovery_score_daily` | day | mean | score | higher_better | `fact_observation`(resting_hr, hrv) | 恢复度 0-100 |
| `subject.deep_sleep_ratio_daily` | day | mean | ratio | higher_better | `fact_observation.deep_sleep_hours` ÷ sleep_hours | 深睡占比 |
| `subject.avg_resting_hr_7d` | day | mean | count | lower_better | `fact_observation.resting_hr` | 近 7 日静息心率均值 |

## B. 活动与专注（5 个）

| metric_id | grain | agg | unit | direction | 数据来源 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `subject.daily_steps` | day | mean | count | higher_better | `fact_observation.steps` | 日步数 |

| `subject.screen_minutes_daily` | day | mean | minute | lower_better | `fact_observation.screen_minutes` | 屏幕时间 |

## C. 财务与情绪（6 个）

| metric_id | grain | agg | unit | direction | 数据来源 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `subject.spending_daily` | day | sum | currency | neutral | `fact_event.amount`(type=transaction) | 日支出 |
| `subject.spending_monthly` | month | sum | currency | neutral | upstream: `spending_daily` | 月支出 |
| `subject.discretionary_spending_ratio` | month | mean | ratio | lower_better | `fact_event.discretionary_spending` ÷ `fact_event.spending`（分类集合固化在 `semantic_models/event.yml`：dining / entertainment / shopping） | 非必要支出占比 |
| `subject.mood_score_daily` | day | mean | score | higher_better | `fact_observation.mood_score` | 情绪 0-100（含季节项） |
| `subject.mood_volatility_7d` | day | mean | score | lower_better | upstream: `mood_score_daily` | 近 7 日情绪标准差 |
| `subject.note_count_weekly` | week | sum | count | neutral | `fact_event`(type=note) | 周笔记数 |

| --- | --- |
